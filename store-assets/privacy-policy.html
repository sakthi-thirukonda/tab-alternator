Tab Alternator - Privacy Policy

TL;DR: We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

What we collect:

  • Tab URLs and domain names
  • Tab titles
  • Visit timestamps
  • Visit counts

What we DON'T collect:

  • Personal information
  • Passwords or login data
  • Form data or input
  • Page content
  • Browsing history beyond tab switching

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. No data is sent to external servers or third parties.

Data Usage

The collected data is used solely to provide tab switching functionality. We do not:

  • Analyze your browsing patterns
  • Create user profiles
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use data for advertising

Data Retention

Data is automatically deleted after 30 days (configurable). You can manually clear all data at any time through the extension settings.

Your Rights

  • Export your data
  • Delete all data
  • Uninstall the extension (removes all data)
  • Configure data retention settings

Permissions

The extension requests minimal permissions:

  • tabs: Required for tab switching functionality
  • storage: Required for local data persistence

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the Chrome Web Store or create an issue in the extension repository.
